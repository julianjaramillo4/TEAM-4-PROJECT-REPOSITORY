## 1. Project Information
- **Software name:** FlowDesk — Order and Cash Management System for Small Businesses.
- **Development company:** Runtime Dynamics.
- **Development team:** Cristopher Lanchimba, Cristopher Gualotuña, and Julián Jaramillo.
- **Initial context:** A print shop that manages custom orders, deliveries, and payments.

## 2. Problem Statement
At the print shop, orders, deliveries, income, and expenses are not well organized. This makes it hard to track jobs, check unpaid balances, and find information quickly.

FlowDesk will keep customer details, orders, and payments in one system to make each order easier to track.

## 3. Product Overview and Conditions
FlowDesk will manage customers, products, services, orders, and cash records. Each order will have a unique number, customer details, order information, and one of five statuses: Waiting, Approved, In Progress, Finished, or Delivered.
The dashboard will show orders by status, and a side menu will make the system easy to use. It will also record optional deposits, cash or bank transfer payments, and unpaid balances. Reports will be available by day, week, and month.
The system will use Object-Oriented Programming (OOP). It will have a simple interface and only the main features, without advanced accounting.
## 4. Background
In a print shop, each order goes through several steps: request, approval, production, delivery, and payment. When records are kept separately, it is hard to check the status and history of each job.
FlowDesk will bring these steps and payments together in one application. It is an information system for managing the daily work and administration of a print shop. It may also be used by similar businesses.
## 5. Main Features
5.1. Five Product Features
Feature
What FlowDesk Will Do
Customer Management
Add, search, and update customers and link them to their orders.
Products and Services
Keep a simple catalog with names, categories, and descriptions.
Work Orders
Create numbered orders, update their five statuses, and track them on the dashboard.
Cash and Payments
Record income, expenses, optional deposits, payment methods, and balances.
Reports
View daily, weekly, and monthly summaries of orders and cash records.
